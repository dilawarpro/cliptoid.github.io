What this watermark remover can and cannot do

Cropping can hide a TikTok logo or watermark placed at the edge of a video, but it also trims that part of the picture. This tool does not remove a watermark from the middle of a frame, fill in missing pixels, or guarantee a particular result. For a clean original, use the unwatermarked source file from your own editing workflow when available.

Only edit videos you created or have permission to modify. Removing a watermark does not grant rights to the video or permission to repost it.

Common questions

TikTok watermark remover FAQs

How can I remove a TikTok watermark from my video?

Choose your video, adjust the crop edges until the watermark is outside the preview, then select Export cropped video. This tool crops the frame instead of erasing or reconstructing pixels.

Can I remove a TikTok watermark online for free?

Yes. Cliptoid's browser cropper is free to use. It processes the selected video locally, subject to your browser's video and recording support.

Does this remove the TikTok logo without cropping?

No. It removes an edge watermark from the visible output by cropping away that part of the frame. The crop may reduce the video dimensions or cut off image content.

Will it remove a moving or centered watermark?

Not reliably. You can crop edge positions, but the tool cannot erase a watermark that moves into the remaining picture or sits in the middle of the video.

Is my uploaded video private?

The file is selected from your device and handled in the browser. Cliptoid does not upload the video to a server. Closing the page clears the local preview.

Why is my video exported as WebM?

WebM is recorded using your browser's built-in media recorder. Browser and device support varies, and this tool does not convert exports to MP4.

Can I use a TikTok watermark remover on someone else's video?

Only edit content you own or have permission to modify. A watermark can identify a creator, and removing it does not transfer copyright or reuse rights.
