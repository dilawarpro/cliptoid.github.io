Download TikTok videos on iPhone

Use Safari to check a TikTok post link and save the video, sound, or slideshow images the post makes available. Cliptoid does not need your TikTok password or an iPhone app install.

Steps for saving a TikTok file on iPhone

  1. In TikTok, open the post, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Open Cliptoid in Safari and paste the link into the downloader.
  3. Tap Download to review the available results. Choose the video, audio, or slideshow image you need.
  4. When Safari starts the download, find the file in the browser download list or the Files app under Downloads.

Save a slideshow photo on iPhone

For a photo post, Cliptoid shows individual image results when the resolver can access them. Tap the save button beneath each image. The post may not return every image, and private or unavailable posts cannot be fetched.

For audio-only results, see the TikTok to MP3 guide. A downloaded file does not grant permission to reuse a creator's work.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download TikTok videos on iPhone?

Yes, when the post and resolver return a video file. Open Cliptoid in Safari, paste the post link, and select the available video result.

Where does Safari save the file?

Safari normally saves downloads in the Downloads folder in the Files app. The exact location follows your iPhone and Safari settings.

Do I need to install an app or sign in to TikTok?

No extra app or TikTok login is required. Cliptoid works in Safari from a post link.
