TikTok download not working? Try these checks

A failed result can come from the link, post access, the media service, or your browser. Work through these checks before trying another tool.

Check the link and post

  1. Open the post in TikTok and copy its link again. Avoid copying a profile, comment, or unrelated share URL.
  2. Confirm that the post is still available to you. Private, friends-only, removed, age-restricted, or region-restricted posts may not resolve.
  3. Paste the link into Cliptoid and wait for the results. Only formats returned for that post can be downloaded.

Check the browser download

  • Open your browser's download panel and check whether the file is paused, blocked, or already saved.
  • Review browser download settings. If it asks where to save files, complete that prompt.
  • Retry on a stable connection with an up-to-date browser. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same link while a request is still running.

When a fresh link still fails

Media links can expire and the third-party resolver can temporarily fail. Try again later. Cliptoid does not bypass login, privacy, or access restrictions.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my TikTok link not working?

The link may be incomplete, expired, private, restricted, removed, or unsupported. Copy it again from the post and retry.

Why does the download button not save a file?

Check the browser download list, download permissions, connection, and whether the browser is asking for a save location. The result can also fail if its media link expired.

Can Cliptoid download a private TikTok post?

No. Cliptoid does not log in or bypass privacy settings. It can only show media the resolver can access.
