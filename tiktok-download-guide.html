TikTok download guide

Follow these steps to check a post link and save the video, sound, or slideshow image options the service returns. A format is not guaranteed for every post.

1. Copy the post link

Open a post you are allowed to access. In TikTok, tap Share and choose Copy link. Do not submit a password or private message.

2. Review the results

Paste the URL into Cliptoid and select Download. Review the returned post and choose an available video, audio, or image result.

3. Find the saved file

Your browser manages the download location. Check its download panel or the Downloads folder configured on your device. If a file will not play, follow the playback troubleshooting guide.
