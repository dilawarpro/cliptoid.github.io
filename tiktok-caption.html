Copy a TikTok video caption online

Found a memorable quote, story, or helpful tip in a TikTok caption? Copying caption text from the app can be awkward. Paste the video link into the caption copier above, review the result, and use Copy caption when caption text is available. Cliptoid can also show a separate copy option for returned hashtags.

1. Copy the TikTok link

Open a video you can access, tap Share, and copy its link.

2. Check the caption

Paste the link into the box above and select Get Caption. Cliptoid checks the post for available text.

3. Copy the text

When a caption is returned, select Copy caption. Use the separate hashtags control if hashtags are available.

Why use a TikTok caption copier?

Captions can add context to a video, share a recipe, tell a story, or include a line you want to keep. A TikTok caption copier makes available text easier to copy without retyping it. Use the text in your personal notes or other permitted ways, and respect the creator’s rights.

Can I download or copy a TikTok caption?

Cliptoid lets you copy caption text returned for a TikTok link; it does not create a separate caption file. Paste the copied text into your notes or document. Caption availability depends on the post and the information returned by the service.

Frequently asked questions

Can I copy the caption from any TikTok video?

Not always. Cliptoid can display caption text only when the post is accessible and caption data is returned. Private, deleted, restricted, or unsupported posts may not work.

Do I need to sign in to copy a caption?

No TikTok password is requested. Use a post link and never enter your account credentials into the tool.

Why is the TikTok caption missing?

The post may be unavailable, restricted, or unsupported, or its caption data may not be returned. Try a different link; results are not guaranteed for every post.

Can I copy hashtags too?

When hashtags are returned, Cliptoid displays them separately so you can copy them independently from the caption.

Can I reuse a copied caption?

Copying text does not grant permission to republish it. Follow applicable laws and platform terms, and get permission from the creator where required.
