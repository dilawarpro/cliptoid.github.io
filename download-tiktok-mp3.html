What is a TikTok to MP3 converter?

A TikTok to MP3 converter saves the sound of a TikTok post as an audio file. Paste the post’s link in the box above and press Download. If the post offers audio, Cliptoid shows a Download Audio MP3 button. It is free, needs no account or app, and works in your browser on iPhone, Android and PC.

The audio comes from the same link as the video, so you do not need a separate tool for it. If you want the video too, use the main TikTok downloader.

How to convert TikTok to MP3

  1. In TikTok, open the post, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Paste the link into the box at the top of this page and press Download.
  3. When the audio option appears, press Download Audio MP3.
  4. Save the file when your browser asks. Where it goes depends on your browser and device settings.

Yes, if the post returns audio. Many popular tracks are licensed music, and saving the file does not give you the right to reuse it in your own videos, ads or streams. Keep it for personal listening, or get permission from the rights holder.

MP3 or M4A: which file will you get?

It depends on what TikTok provides for that post. Some audio arrives as MP3 and some as M4A, an audio format used inside MP4 files. Most phones, computers and media players can play M4A. If a device or app insists on MP3, a free player such as VLC can convert the file. Quality follows the original sound, and converting a file does not make it sound better.

Set a TikTok sound as a ringtone

A saved TikTok sound can be a ringtone on your own phone. On Android, open Settings, go to Sound and choose Phone ringtone, then pick the saved file. Menu names vary by phone brand. An iPhone is less direct: ringtones must be short clips in iPhone’s own ringtone format, so you need to trim and convert the file first, for example with GarageBand or from a computer. Keep this to personal use unless you have the rights to the sound.

TikTok to MP3 not working? Try these fixes

  • The post is private, deleted or restricted. If you cannot open the post without logging in, Cliptoid usually cannot get audio from it either.
  • The link is a short link. Open it in your browser first, then copy the full address from the address bar and paste that instead.
  • The media link has expired. Media URLs can expire, so retry later or copy the link again from TikTok.
  • The post has no audio result. Availability depends on the post and the third-party resolver. Try another post that uses the same sound.
  • The page is not responding. Reload it, turn off an ad blocker or VPN for a moment, and update your browser.

If the link itself fails, see TikTok download troubleshooting.

Is it legal to download TikTok audio?

Only download, copy, or reuse audio when you own it or have permission and your use complies with applicable law and platform terms. Many TikTok sounds are licensed music. Saving a file for personal listening is different from putting it in a video, an ad or a stream. Fair use rules differ by country; the US Copyright Office explains the idea on its fair use page, and TikTok’s own rules are in its Terms of Service. Businesses that need music for ads should look at TikTok’s Commercial Music Library. This is general information, not legal advice.
