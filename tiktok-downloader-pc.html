Download TikTok Videos on PC or Mac

Cliptoid runs in a desktop browser. Paste a TikTok post address to check for available video, audio, and slideshow files before saving.

Steps for a desktop browser

  1. Open the TikTok post you are allowed to access. Copy its address from the browser bar, or use TikTok's Share menu and choose Copy link.
  2. Paste the link into the downloader above and select Download.
  3. Review the preview. Choose an available MP4, MP3, or image result.
  4. Find the saved file in your browser's download panel or its configured Downloads folder.

When the download does not start

Check whether your browser is set to ask for a save location, blocked the download, or paused it. If the result itself is missing, copy a fresh post link and try again. Cliptoid only offers files returned for that link.

Frequently asked questions

How do I download a TikTok video on PC?

Copy the public post address, paste it into Cliptoid, select Download, then choose the video file if one is returned.

Where does the downloaded file go on Windows or Mac?

Your browser handles the download location. Check its downloads list and the Downloads folder configured in browser settings.

Can I download a private TikTok post?

No. Cliptoid does not log in or bypass privacy controls. Private, removed, restricted, or unsupported posts may not be available.
