About Cliptoid

Cliptoid is an independent, browser-based utility for checking links to public TikTok posts. A result may include a video preview, an audio option, slideshow images, or copyable caption and hashtag text. The formats shown depend on what is available for the individual post.

A link-based workflow

Start by copying the URL of a public post. Cliptoid checks that link and organizes the returned media and text into separate choices, so you can review the preview before saving a file. The tool does not require a TikTok password, account search, or browser extension.

Availability can vary

Public links can still be unavailable if a post has been removed, restricted, or is not supported. Media addresses may expire, and not every post provides every format or the same quality. Cliptoid cannot override TikTok access settings or guarantee a specific result.

Privacy and responsible use

Cliptoid does not ask for a TikTok password. Use only public links and only save or reuse material when you have the rights or permission to do so. Review the Privacy Policy for information about third-party processing.
