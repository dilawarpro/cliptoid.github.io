Check a TikTok post for slideshow images

Some public TikTok posts are photo slideshows. Cliptoid can display and offer individual image downloads when the media service returns slideshow images for a post. Availability varies, and this tool cannot access private or restricted posts.

How to check for slideshow images

  1. Copy the link to a public TikTok slideshow you are allowed to access.
  2. Paste the link above and select Download.
  3. If images are returned, review the preview and save images individually.

Stories and post availability
