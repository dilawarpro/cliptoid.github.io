TikTok video won't play after download?

A successful download does not guarantee that every built-in player supports the file's video and audio codecs. Use these checks to distinguish an incomplete file from a player compatibility issue.

Check that the download completed

  1. Check the browser's download panel for a failed or incomplete transfer.
  2. Compare the file size with any earlier attempt. A tiny file may be an error page rather than a video.
  3. Copy a fresh TikTok post link and download the result again if the media URL may have expired.

Check the media player

Try a current player that supports a wider range of codecs, such as VLC obtained from its official website. Keep your operating system and media apps updated. Do not install codec packs from unfamiliar download sites.
