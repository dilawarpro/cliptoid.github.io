How to choose a TikTok downloader

  • Use a service that previews the post before you save a file.
  • Do not enter your TikTok password or install software you do not trust.
  • Check whether a video, audio, or slideshow option is actually returned for your link.
  • Read the privacy policy and confirm where the browser saves downloads.
  • Respect creator rights and use only content you own or have permission to save.
