Three ways to download a video on TikTok, compared

People usually try TikTok’s own Save button first, then look for something else when it does not work. Here is how the options actually differ, so you can pick the one that fits.

TikTok’s Save video button

Built into the app and needs no extra tool. The catch: it only appears when the creator allows downloads, and the saved file carries TikTok’s logo and the creator’s username.

Screen recording

Works when nothing else does, but it records everything on screen, including buttons and captions, and the quality is lower than the original file. Best kept as a last resort.

A link downloader like Cliptoid

Paste the link and choose from the files returned for that post. You get a preview first, and the video normally comes without the logo overlay. It only works for public posts.

Why is the Save video option missing on some TikToks?

If you open the share menu and Save video is not there, one of these is usually the reason:

  • The creator turned off downloads for that video or account.
  • The account or post is private, or limited to certain viewers.
  • The video was deleted or is not available in your region.
  • Your TikTok app is out of date and the menu looks different.

In the first and last cases you can still copy the link and use the box at the top of this page, as long as the post is public. Private and removed posts cannot be saved by any downloader, and Cliptoid does not try to get around privacy settings.

Download a TikTok video on iPhone

iPhone does not allow web apps in TikTok’s share menu, so the copy-and-paste method is the way to go. It is only a few taps:

  1. In TikTok, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Open Safari, go to cliptoid.com, paste the link and press Download.
  3. Choose MP4 and tap Download when Safari asks.
  4. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, open the video and tap the share icon, then Save Video to move it to Photos.

Download a TikTok video on PC or Mac

Open the video on tiktok.com, copy the page address from the address bar, paste it in the box above and press Download. It works the same in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. There is no emulator to install and nothing to record. Chrome keeps your saved files in its downloads panel, which opens with Ctrl+J.

Where does a downloaded TikTok video go?

Your browser decides, not Cliptoid. On Android, check the browser’s download list or the Downloads folder in your Files app. On iPhone, look in Files, then Downloads. On a computer, look in your Downloads folder or the browser’s downloads panel. If you cannot find it, search your device for the file name or sort Downloads by date.

Can you download a TikTok video without the watermark?

Usually, yes. The logo and username you see on videos saved with TikTok’s own button are added by the app when it exports the file. A downloader that works from the link offers the version returned for that post, which normally has no overlay. The one exception is a logo that is part of the picture itself, for example a clip that someone already reposted with their own mark on it. That cannot be removed by any downloader. What video quality will you get?

The quality is whatever TikTok provides for that post. When an HD version exists, an HD button appears next to the standard one, and when it does not, you only see the standard option. If a saved video looks soft, the original upload was probably low resolution. Re-downloading will not improve it.

Need audio, photos or a caption instead?

The same link gives you other formats when the post makes them available. Save the sound with the TikTok to MP3 guide, grab slideshow photos one by one through the slideshow and image guide, or copy a caption and its hashtags with the TikTok caption copier.

Download not working? Try these quick fixes

  • Copy the link again. Media links expire, and a fresh link fixes most failures.
  • Check that the post is public. Private, deleted and restricted videos cannot be fetched.
  • Paste only the link. Extra text before or after it can cause a “wrong link” message.
  • Try another browser or turn off extensions that block scripts on the page.
  • Wait a minute and try again if TikTok is slow to return the file.
