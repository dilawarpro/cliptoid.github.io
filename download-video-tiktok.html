Three ways to download a video on TikTok, compared

Most people try TikTok’s own Save button first, then look for something else when it is missing. Here is how the options really differ, so you can pick the one that fits.

TikTok’s Save video button

Built into the app, so it needs no extra tool. It only appears when the creator allows downloads, and the saved file carries TikTok’s logo and the creator’s username.

Screen recording

Works when nothing else does, but it also records buttons and captions, and the quality is lower than the original file. Keep it as a last resort.

A link downloader like Cliptoid

Paste the link and choose from the files returned for that post. You see a preview first, and the video normally has no logo overlay. It only works for public posts.

Why is the Save video option missing on some TikToks?

  • The creator turned off downloads for that video or account.
  • The account or post is private, or limited to certain viewers.
  • The video was deleted or is not available in your region.
  • Your TikTok app is out of date and the menu looks different.

In the first and last cases you can still copy the link and use the box at the top of this page, as long as the post is public. Private and removed posts cannot be saved by any downloader, and Cliptoid does not try to get around privacy settings.

Download a TikTok video on iPhone

An iPhone does not let web apps appear in TikTok’s share menu, so copy and paste is the way to go:

  1. In TikTok, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Open Safari, go to cliptoid.com, paste the link and press Download.
  3. Choose MP4 and tap Download when Safari asks.
  4. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, open the video and tap the share icon, then Save Video to move it to Photos.

Download a TikTok video on PC or Mac

Open the video on tiktok.com, copy the address from the address bar, paste it in the box above and press Download. It works the same in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Nothing needs installing. In Chrome, your saved files appear in the downloads panel, which opens with Ctrl+J.

Where does the downloaded video go?

Your browser decides, not Cliptoid. On Android, check the browser’s download list or the Downloads folder in Files. On iPhone, look in Files, then Downloads. On a computer, open your Downloads folder. If you cannot find it, sort Downloads by date.

“This video isn’t available in the server’s region”: what it means

Some TikTok posts are limited to certain countries. Cliptoid requests the file from its own server, so a post that is restricted from where that server sits can fail even though it plays fine on your phone. Here is what to try, in order:

  1. Open the post in the browser and copy the full address (it looks like tiktok.com/@name/video/…) instead of a short vt.tiktok.com link, then paste that.
  2. Wait a few minutes and try again. Availability can change.
  3. Open the post in a private browser tab. If it will not play without logging in, it is restricted, and no link downloader can save it.
  4. If a public post keeps failing, send the link through the contact page so it can be checked.

Can you download a TikTok video without the watermark?

Usually, yes. The logo and username on videos saved with TikTok’s own button are added by the app when it exports the file. A downloader that works from the link offers the version returned for that post, which normally has no overlay. The exception is a logo that is part of the picture itself, such as a clip someone already reposted with their own mark. No downloader can remove that. The TikTok watermark remover guide explains what is and is not possible.

What video quality will you get?

The quality is whatever TikTok provides for that post. When an HD version exists, an HD button appears next to the standard one. If a saved video looks soft, the original upload was probably low resolution, and downloading it again will not improve it.

Download not working? Try these quick fixes

  • Copy the link again. Media links expire, and a fresh link fixes most failures.
  • Check that the post is public. Private, deleted and restricted videos cannot be fetched.
  • Paste only the link. Extra text before or after it can trigger a “wrong link” message.
  • Try another browser or turn off extensions that block scripts on the page.
  • Wait a minute and try again if TikTok is slow to return the file.
