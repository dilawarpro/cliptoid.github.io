TikTok Slideshow and Photo Downloader

Save the photos from a TikTok slideshow

Some TikTok posts are photo slideshows with music playing over them. Paste the post's link and Cliptoid lists each image it can find, so you can save the ones you want one by one. Whether images appear depends on the post, and private or restricted posts cannot be fetched.

How to save TikTok slideshow images

  1. In TikTok, open the slideshow, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Paste the link into the box at the top of this page and press Download.
  3. Check the preview and press the download button under each image you want.
  4. Save the files when your browser asks. On iPhone, tap Download when Safari asks and find the images in the Files app, under Downloads.

Looking for TikTok stories?

TikTok stories expire after 24 hours and are often restricted, so they are usually not available through a link. Photo posts and slideshows have regular post links, and they are what this page is built for. Cliptoid cannot guarantee that any post will stay available.

Common questions

Can I download a TikTok story or slideshow?

If the media service returns slideshow or story images for the link, you can preview them and save the individual images. Some posts or stories are not available because they are private, removed, or restricted. On iPhone, see the iPhone saving guide.

Why does the slideshow option disappear?

Only some posts return slideshow image files. If the link is private, deleted, expired, or unsupported, the result may be blank or incomplete. Try a fresh link and check again later, or use these download troubleshooting steps.

Do I need a separate tool for MP3 or video?

No. Use the main TikTok downloader for video checks and the TikTok MP3 guide for audio-only downloads when those formats are returned.
