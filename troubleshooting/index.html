TikTok download troubleshooting

Start with the guide that matches what happened. Cliptoid can only process posts the media service can access, and playback depends on the file returned and your device.

A TikTok download is not working

Check the copied link, post availability, browser download settings, and common resolver limits.

A downloaded video will not play

Check whether a transfer completed and whether your media player supports the file's codecs.

A post is private or unavailable

Learn why a link-based downloader cannot access private or restricted posts.

Still stuck?

Copy a fresh link from a post you are allowed to access and try the Cliptoid downloader again. Do not share a TikTok password or session cookie with a downloader. If the problem continues, use the contact page to report it.
