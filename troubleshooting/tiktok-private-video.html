Can you download a private TikTok video?

No. A link-based downloader cannot access content that TikTok keeps private or restricted without bypassing the creator's settings. Cliptoid does not log in to accounts or circumvent those controls.

Why private links do not work

Private, friends-only, removed, age-restricted, or region-restricted posts may not be visible to the media service used to check a link. A URL by itself does not grant permission to view or save the post.

If the post is yours

Use TikTok's own account tools and settings to access your content. If you have the original video saved on your device, use that copy. Do not share a password or session cookie with a downloader.

If you have permission to access the post

Confirm the link is copied from the post itself and that the post is available to the account and region using it. If access is allowed but the link still fails, try a fresh URL later. See TikTok download troubleshooting.

Public post, but another downloader fails? Try Cliptoid

If the post is public and another downloader will not load it, copy a fresh link and try the Cliptoid homepage. You get a preview first, and no TikTok login or password is needed.
