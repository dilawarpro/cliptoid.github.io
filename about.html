About Cliptoid

Cliptoid is an independent, browser-based utility for checking links to TikTok posts. A result may include a video preview, an audio option, slideshow images, or copyable caption and hashtag text. The formats shown depend on what is available for the individual post.

Who builds Cliptoid

Cliptoid is built and maintained by Dilawar, an independent developer with no connection to TikTok or ByteDance. You can write to me directly at businesswithdilawar@gmail.com.

Ways to use Cliptoid

Paste a link into the TikTok downloader without watermark on any device, or install Cliptoid on Android and send videos to it from TikTok's share menu. There are separate guides for TikTok MP3, slideshow photos and the caption copier. See the iPhone guide or PC and Mac guide for device-specific steps.

A link-based workflow

Start by copying the URL of a post. Cliptoid checks that link and organizes the returned media and text into separate choices, so you can review the preview before saving a file. The tool does not require a TikTok password, account search, or browser extension.

Availability can vary

Links can still be unavailable if a post has been removed, restricted, or is not supported. Media addresses may expire, and not every post provides every format or the same quality. Cliptoid cannot override TikTok access settings or guarantee a specific result.

Privacy and responsible use

Cliptoid does not ask for a TikTok password. Use only links and only save or reuse material when you have the rights or permission to do so. Review the Privacy Policy for information about third-party processing.
