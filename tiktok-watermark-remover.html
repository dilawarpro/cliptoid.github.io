    How this TikTok watermark remover works

    TikTok adds a logo and username to videos saved with its built-in download option. The mark is part of the picture, so it cannot be switched off like a layer. A watermark remover has to repaint, blur, or cut away those pixels. This tool gives you four ways to do that and lets you combine them.

    Smart fill

    Smart fill rebuilds each marked box from the pixels around its edges, blending them toward the centre. It works best when the background behind the watermark is a plain wall, sky, or soft gradient. On busy detail such as text or patterned clothing, you may see a smudge.

    Blur

    Blur softens the marked area into a low-detail patch. It is quicker to read as intentional than a smeared fill, and it is a reasonable choice when the watermark sits over a complex background.

    Crop

    Cropping removes a strip from the top, bottom, left, or right. It is the cleanest option when the mark sits near an edge, because nothing has to be guessed. The trade-off is a smaller frame, so keep an eye on faces and captions.

    Trim end card

    Many saved TikToks finish with a short end card showing the logo and username. Enter the number of seconds to cut and the new file stops before it.
