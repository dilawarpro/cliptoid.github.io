Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information involved when you use Cliptoid. Cliptoid is a browser-based tool and does not request your TikTok password.

Links and media processing

When you submit a TikTok post URL, your browser sends that URL to TikWM, a third-party service, to request available post information. Your browser may then request returned preview or media files from the provider’s media hosts. These services receive requests under their own privacy policies and may process technical data such as IP address, browser information, and request time. Do not submit private or sensitive URLs.

Technical information, analytics, and advertising

Our hosting provider and any analytics or advertising services enabled on the site may process technical information, cookies, or similar identifiers to operate, measure, protect, or fund the service. Third-party providers determine their own data handling. Browser settings can be used to control cookies, though some site functions may be affected. Where required, advertising and analytics technologies should be enabled only after obtaining valid consent.

Retention and choices

Cliptoid does not ask you to create an account to use the downloader. We do not control the retention practices of hosting, media, analytics, or advertising providers. You can avoid submitting a link, close the page, or use browser privacy controls. Contact the relevant provider for requests about information it controls.

Children and policy updates

Cliptoid is not designed to collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the service or its providers change; check this page for the current version.
