Cliptoid is a link-processing tool. It does not claim ownership of TikTok posts or the media returned for a link. Content remains the responsibility of its creator and rights holders.

Before submitting a request

If you believe that Cliptoid's own materials infringe your rights, or that a result exposes material that should not be available, contact us through the contact page. Include enough information for us to understand the issue without sending passwords or other sensitive information.

Information that helps us review a concern

  • The specific Cliptoid page or public post URL involved.
  • A description of the material and the right or privacy concern.
  • Your name and a reliable way to reply, plus your relationship to the rights holder if you are acting for someone else.
  • The action you are requesting and a statement that the information you provide is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

Cliptoid may need to refer a request to the relevant platform or media provider because it does not control their source files or availability. We will review requests concerning materials and pages under Cliptoid's control.
