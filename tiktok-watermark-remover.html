How to get a TikTok video without a watermark

Use a link to a post you can access. Cliptoid checks the link and displays the media options the resolver returns. If a video without a watermark is available, choose that option in the results and save it through your browser.

  1. In TikTok, open the post you are allowed to save and choose Share, then Copy link.
  2. Paste the copied URL into the field above and select Check link.
  3. Review the returned preview and formats. Select the available video download option if it meets your needs.
  4. Save the file when your browser prompts you. Its download location depends on your browser and device settings.

TikTok watermark remover FAQ

How do I remove a watermark from a TikTok video?

Copy the link to an accessible post, paste it into the field above, and check the returned formats. If a watermark-free video is available, select it to download. This workflow does not remove a watermark from a local video file.

Does this tool upload my video?

You submit a post link rather than an uploaded video file. Cliptoid does not ask for your TikTok password. The resolver checks the URL and returns the media options it can access.

Can I remove a watermark from any TikTok post?

No. Results depend on whether the post and its media are accessible to the resolver. Private, deleted, restricted, or unsupported posts may not return a video.

Is the TikTok watermark remover free?

Cliptoid is free to use. The media formats and quality returned can vary by post, and availability is not guaranteed.

Can I use a downloaded video as my own?

No. Downloading a copy does not transfer copyright or other rights. Get permission from the rights holder before reposting or otherwise reusing someone else's video.

What if I already have a video file?

This page checks TikTok post links; it does not accept or edit local files. If you have the original video, use your editing software or obtain a clean export from the creator.
